A calmer way to
make the day count.

Bring the craft and originality of award-level digital work to a student planner: a recognizable point of view, careful type and rhythm, and interactions that feel considered—while keeping the product easy to use between classes.

Design briefKeep the app’s mobile-first planner structure, blue-gray light/dark themes, Today dashboard, quick capture, and desktop planning navigation. Evolve its visual voice without borrowing a particular Awwwards site or disrupting everyday utility.
